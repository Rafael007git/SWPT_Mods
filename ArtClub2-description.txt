ART CLUB 2 mod description
=============================================

This is a more advanced and expanded version of my ART CLUB mod. What's inside:

1. Rotating disco ball with coloured lights. Three versions included: static, rotating, rotating+fog.
2. Easel with painting. Canvas displays custom image from a .png file located in the mod folder. You can replace that file with your custom image to see your picture on the canvas in game (see the detailed instructions in Readme.txt). Two variations are present: with vertical and horizontal canvas.
3. Painting brush (as dagger) and palette (as shield). You can equip those to pose near the paintings. (Unfortunately the palette can be added only by editing a save file, which is a known problem with all shields in game)
4. Microphone on a stand. Two variations: silver and golden.
5. Piano and harp. Improved versions from my old ART CLUB mod with better details and more accurate colliders.
6. Pole dancing stage. Also improved from my old ART CLUB (better design and more accurate colliders allowing you actually walk up and down the steps). Two variations: wooden with gold, dark marble with silver.

I am also working on poses and animations for all these furniture items. However due to the limitations of the game engine they can work only with BepInEx version of this mod. I am going to publish it at Nexusmods later and inform you in the comment section here!
